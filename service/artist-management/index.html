NOAH HUNTON

Home is where you kick your boots off, and for Noah Hunton that home is in Ocala, FL. Naturally, being in the south there is a large influence of country music and with his voice and innate ability to strum the guitar, he was destined for the genre. Noah has been playing solo acoustic concerts and full band shows at local venues on the weekends all over the east coast, opening for known musicians, and performing at festivals, weddings, and private events.

MICHAEL CARUBELLI

Michael is a Texas-born singer-songwriter whose sound blends modern country energy with true storytelling. Having shared the stage with legends like Clay Walker, Shenandoah, and Wade Bowen, Michael has earned a reputation for delivering powerful, crowd-pleasing performances with vocals you won’t forget. is newest single, My Dad Builds Homes, showcases his heartfelt songwriting and continues to build on a strong catalog that includes fan favorites like “Tell Me” & “Drunken Princess.”
